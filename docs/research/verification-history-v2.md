# v2 verification history: reviews, economy analyses, playtests, fixes


## Browser playtest

**fun notes:** The core loop (tap die, watch payout float above it, buy +SIDE to grow the die's geometry) is immediately legible and satisfying. Growing from a 1-sided blob through coin, triangle, square, pentagon, hexagon into a proper cube is a genuinely charming, show-don't-tell way to teach the mechanic without a tutorial. The skins system does exactly what the owner asked: a one-screen grid, no sub-menus, and equipping a skin visibly recolors the die and background instantly.

**pacing notes:** Early game (1-6 sides) is fast and satisfying, one tap = clearly one number = one payout. Once ROLLER (auto-roll) unlocks at 6 sides, manual taps and the passive roller fire concurrently and their floating +N payout texts stack and overlap above the die, becoming unreadable within a second or two of idle play. This will only get worse as the owner adds more automation for the "years of play" late game.

**legibility notes:** 1-6 sided dice are excellent: every face is numbered 1..N exactly as the owner wanted, and the landed face lines up under the orange pointer with a matching payout number. From roughly 20+ sides the game switches to a dial/clock design that shows only the landed number in a plate rather than every face, which is a good scalable solution and stayed legible up to 134 sides in testing. However at higher counts I observed rendering hiccups where the numeral inside the plate briefly failed to update/render blank while the needle still moved. The x2 side and jackpot-style bonus faces (bought via the "×2 SIDE" upgrade) are not marked anywhere on the dial face or the tick marks, so there is no way to see which numbered face is the double before or after it lands, other than noticing the payout was higher than the shown face by chance.

**mobile layout notes:** At 375x812 in both light and dark themes I did not find overlapping UI elements in the normal play state; the settings modal, skins modal, and dev-tools row all stayed within the viewport and were tap-friendly. The one layout-adjacent defect is not overlap but invisibility: the "HOT HAND" status chip's DOM container has `display:none`/zero-size in the state I could reach, so even if the buff is active there may be no visible on-screen indication of it at all (see findings).

**persistence notes:** Reload correctly restored currency (2.00B), die side-count (134), equipped skin (orange die), and theme (dark) — full state persisted across reload via the single localStorage key `oms.save.v2`. No v1/legacy save key existed in this environment (only `oms.save.v2` was present), so I could not observe what happens to a pre-existing v1 save; that migration path is untested and should be checked directly against a real device that has an old save.

**Best moments:**
- The die's shape itself growing from a lopsided blob -> lens/coin (2 sides) -> triangle (3) -> square (4) -> pentagon (5) -> hexagon (6) -> circle-with-dial (high sides) is a delightful, wordless way to teach 'more sides = more numbers' exactly per the owner's show-don't-tell brief.
- Landed face and payout number matching up cleanly under the orange pointer on every die from 1 to ~6 sides, with the payout appearing directly above the die as requested.
- The SKINS panel: a single simple 3x2 grid split into two categories, each showing an explicit 'X/3 - complete for +10% income' progress line, with instant equip-and-see-the-change feedback (die visibly turned orange immediately).

**Findings:**
- [major] **Hot Hand status chip renders with zero size / display:none** (Hot Hand status chip, header/status area): The DOM node containing the text "HOT HAND ×3 · 120s" exists but its parent container has computed style display:none and a 0x0 bounding rect (confirmed via JS inspection), and even the text node itself is far too small/wrong-colored to read on the light theme (10px, muted gray). Across roughly 90 real interactions including bursts of rapid tapping, I never saw a Hot Hand indicator appear on screen, so a real player has no way to know the buff triggered, what it does, or when it ends -- exactly the gap the owner already suspected. Suggested fix: Give the Hot Hand chip a guaranteed-visible container (no display:none while active), a legible size/contrast in both themes, and a one-line explanation the first time it triggers (e.g. a toast: "Hot Hand! +3x payouts for 120s").
- [major] **No visual marker for which face is the x2/jackpot side** (Die face rendering / ×2 SIDE upgrade, all side counts): After purchasing the "×2 SIDE" upgrade the shop entry reads "1 FACES PAY ×2, +1" but neither the numbered die faces (up to 6 sides) nor the dial ticks (high side counts) show any highlight, color, or icon on the specific face that pays double. The only way to notice the bonus is retroactively, by realizing a payout number was larger than the landed face number. Suggested fix: Render the bonus face with a distinct color/icon on the die itself (matching the request to add a jackpot side that's visually distinct), and briefly flash/highlight it when landed on.
- [major] **Overlapping floating payout text once auto-roll (ROLLER) is active** (Payout floating-text animation, above the die): Once the ROLLER upgrade is purchased, the passive auto-roll and manual taps both spawn a rising/fading "+N" text above the die. With both firing close together the texts overlap and clip against each other (observed e.g. "+18"/"+38" overlaid, and a partially-faded "+28" reading as "+2"), making it impossible to read what any single roll actually paid. Suggested fix: Stagger or stack floating payout texts vertically/horizontally so concurrent rolls (manual + auto) don't render on top of each other, or batch rapid auto-roll payouts into a single running total display.
- [minor] **Numeral briefly fails to render inside the dial plate at very high side counts** (High-side-count dial rendering (~130+ sides)): At 134 sides, one screenshot showed the dial with its needle positioned normally but the numeral plate above it completely empty (no digit), while the very next roll displayed correctly. This looked like an intermittent render/timing glitch rather than a reproducible state. Suggested fix: Log/repro with a longer soak test at high side counts; likely a race between the roll animation finishing and the face-label update.
- [minor] **Settings gear icon uses the same sun glyph as the theme toggle** (Header icons, top-left and top-right): The top-left icon (aria-label "Light or dark") correctly toggles the theme in this v2 build and a full Settings panel (Sound, Theme, Erase-everything, version) now exists behind the top-right gear-looking icon (aria-label "Settings") -- so the core complaint from the owner ("light/dark button doesn't work, goes to empty settings") appears already fixed in this build. The only residual issue is that in light mode both the theme toggle and the Settings icon render as near-identical sun glyphs, which is easy to confuse at a glance. Suggested fix: Give Settings a distinct icon (gear/cog) so it's visually distinguishable from the sun/moon theme toggle.
- [minor] **No v1 legacy save present to verify migration** (localStorage / save migration): localStorage only contained `oms.save.v2`; there was no `oms.save` or similarly named v1 key in this test environment, so the requested check of "what happened to a v1 save" could not be exercised here. Suggested fix: Re-run this specific check on a real device/browser profile that still has a v1 save, or ship a small in-app self-test that seeds a fake v1 key and confirms it migrates or is clearly archived.

The economy is in good shape. Dead zones: zero over 4 minutes on every always-open policy I ran, including the two narrowest ones — sides-only (a player who only ever presses + SIDE: 314 purchases, longest gap 0.1 min) and x2-sides-only (longest 0.3 min). End of content lands at 126.2 min against a 135 target (-6.5%). Precision is a non-issue: peak lifetime coins 6.42e8 against a 9.01e15 limit.

One real dominant strategy exists, and it is the prestige button. Because MAX_RECASTS caps the game at three presses while shardsFor has no ceiling on runCoins/threshold, waiting to 2x threshold finishes content *faster* (122.0 vs 126.2 min) and 28% more powerful (M x9.84 vs x7.68) than pressing when the card lights. It scales all the way to M x31.98 at 32x. That makes the lit RECAST card an instruction to do the worse thing — a "show, don't tell" signal pointing the wrong way. Fix is one clamp (SHARD_RATIO_CAP: 2 inside shardsFor) or, constants-only, SHARD_EXP 0.45 -> 0.25.

Two other things sim.js's bots cannot see. First, + SIDE always auto-targets the fewest-sided die, and COMBO_MIN_SIDES=6 is a cliff, so the 5->6 purchase is always income-negative (-0.23% to -1.19% measured on reachable shapes) and the player cannot route around it; a never-prestige player freezes at 60/59/5 sides for 560 minutes. Second, a jackpot beeline reaches the first jackpot at 4.06 min against a 9-minute target, burning the game's headline reveal four minutes in and then collapsing.

I did check whether ROLLER dominates, since its income-per-coin runs 100-300x everything else late in the run. It does not in practice: a roller-first policy finishes at 126.1 min against cheapest-first's 126.2 — identical. Invariant 3 holds in spirit, not just in the narrow form sim.js asserts.

One note on scope, since it bears on the creed: none of this is fixable by adding shop cards or toggles. The RECAST fix in particular should land as a number the player can watch move on the card they already have, not as a new screen.

**Findings:**
- [blocker] **Hoarding before RECAST strictly dominates pressing it — the button lights at the wrong moment** (index.html <script id="economy">, shardsFor() and K.SHARD_EXP / K.SHARD_COEF / K.MAX_RECASTS; sim.js run() recasts unconditionally at E.unlocked(st,'recast')): shardsFor() is floor(12 * (runCoins/threshold)^0.45) with no ceiling on the ratio, while MAX_RECASTS caps the game at 3 presses. With a fixed number of presses and an unbounded per-press payoff, the only correct play is to maximise coins per press. Sweeping the bank-to multiple with identical buying (1.7 taps/s): press at 1x threshold ends content at 126.2 min with M x7.68 and 1.36e7 coins/min; wait for 2x ends at 122.0 min with M x9.84 and 1.74e7/min. Waiting is faster AND 28% more powerful — dominance on both axes, not a trade-off. It keeps going: 4x -> M x13.08, 8x -> x17.40, 16x -> x23.34, 32x -> x31.98. The RECAST card lighting at exactly 420,000 x M is the game's own 'show, don't tell' signal telling the player to take the worse action, and a player who ignores it is rewarded. This is also the one finding sim.js structurally cannot see: its reference bot recasts the instant unlocked, so every policy it runs sits on the dominated side of this choice. Suggested fix: Saturate the ratio. Add K.SHARD_RATIO_CAP: 2 and change shardsFor to Math.floor(K.SHARD_COEF * Math.pow(Math.min(st.runCoins/threshold, K.SHARD_RATIO_CAP), K.SHARD_EXP)) — 16 shards max per run, 48 total, M x8.2, and there is provably nothing to gain past 2x. Constants-only fallback if you will not touch the function: SHARD_EXP 0.45 -> 0.25, which cuts the 8x hoard edge from 2.27x to 1.56x and makes the hoard cost 30 extra minutes instead of being free. Either way, make the RECAST card print the shard number it will pay and show that number ticking up while the player waits — then the choice is visible instead of secret, which is what 'show, don't tell' means here.
- [major] **+ SIDE auto-targeting makes the 5->6 purchase permanently income-negative, and a no-prestige player freezes at 60/59/5 sides** (index.html <script id="economy">, sideTarget() and K.COMBO_MIN_SIDES; visible in sim.js never-prestige policy output): sideTarget() always picks the die with the fewest sides, and COMBO_MIN_SIDES=6 is a cliff: the moment a small die crosses it, it joins the match and thins it. I measured the single 5->6 purchase directly on reachable shapes: 100/100/5 -> -0.23%, 60/59/5 -> -0.45%, 30/30/5 -> -1.03%, 18/18/5 -> -1.19%. It recovers on the very next side (+0.15%/side) so the total dip is short — but the player cannot choose which die gets the side, so the negative press is forced. Consequence on the played path: never-prestige (which sim.js itself calls 'the single most common idle-game behaviour') locks at 60/59/5 sides for 560 of its 600 simulated minutes, never reaches 100 sides, and never finishes content. sim.js flags this as a WARN with no cause given, and its lattice scan (invariant 1) reports the dip as 'forced + SIDE purchases lose EV, worst 1.71%' without connecting it to the played-path freeze. A real player is not blocked — the card is buyable — but a player watching the coin counter sees income go down on the only button they have, which is exactly what invariant 1 exists to forbid. Suggested fix: Change sideTarget to skip a die sitting at exactly COMBO_MIN_SIDES-1 sides when another die can still grow, so the crossing happens when it is free rather than when it is forced: after picking the fewest-sided die, if its n === K.COMBO_MIN_SIDES - 1 and some other die has n < MAX_SIDES, target that other die instead. That is a 3-line change with no constant retune. Do not fix this by lowering COMBO_MIN_SIDES — that raises the doubles share against invariant 4.
- [major] **A jackpot beeline reaches the first jackpot at 4.06 min against a 9 min target — outside the +/-35% band, and sim.js has no policy that finds it** (index.html <script id="economy">, K.JACK_BASE = 2500 and jackTarget()/unlocked(st,'jack'); sim.js POLICIES array has no jackpot-priority chooser): A player who buys only the sides the jackpot gate demands, the one roller level the unlock demands, and banks everything else gets JACKPOT SIDE at 4.06 min. Spec target is 9 min, tolerance 5.85-12.15, so this is 0.45x — outside the band on the fast side. The jackpot is the game's headline moment (gold face, star, sparkle) and the whole mid-game is supposed to be the x2 track; this path skips it (first x2 at 77 min) and burns the reveal at minute four. The same policy then collapses — 600 min, 2 sides on die 1, income 3.04e3/min, recast 2's income never regained — so it is a genuine trap, not just a speedrun. sim.js's six policies contain no jackpot-priority bot, so nothing currently tests this. Suggested fix: JACK_BASE 2500 -> 4000. ## Code review

**Overall:** I read SPEC.md end to end and all 3,315 lines of index.html, and verified each item below against the exact code path before listing it. No blockers: the core loop is sound. Rolling, x2/jackpot face assignment, doubles/triples, purchasing, Recast and the save sanitiser all hold up under adversarial reading.

Specifically, the things most likely to be broken are not: `faceValue` and the x2 `needs = max(8, k+1)` gate make it structurally impossible for the x2 track to reach the jackpot's face 0; `buy()` re-checks `unlocked` and funds after `costOf`, so there is no insufficient-funds or negative-cost exploit; `landResult` is purely visual and `E.roll` is the only thing that credits coins, so there is no double-credit path through the freeze/panel "quiet" branch; `clampInt`/`num` correctly reject NaN, Infinity and wrong types past the version check; the cost ladders reproduce Appendix A1's published numbers exactly (13/15/17/19/22..., x2 90..184320, tier3 1105920, roller 600..~33.35M) and `jackGate` solves to A4's 12/12/12/19/36/67. The DOUBLES tag now bursts at the midpoint with no connecting line, the ×2/jackpot faces carry tags rather than bare tints, and the theme toggle at #themeBtn is a real persisted toggle wired to `toggleTheme`, not a route into settings — the three things you flagged from the earlier build are genuinely fixed here.

The seven majors cluster into two themes, and both are presentation rather than economy. First, the game tells the player numbers that disagree with what it pays: MAX ROLL is multiplier-free while every float is multiplied (up to ~10x apart), the x2 card names the wrong face, and the offline card's duration and amount drift apart. Second, the feedback layer does not survive its own late game: at roller L11 the haptics, jackpot chime and coin sparkle fire ~58 times a second with no throttle (the floats have one, these do not), floats are dropped rather than merged whenever more than one auto-roll lands in a frame, and the float itself rises off the top of the canvas once a die is height-constrained because `fitScale` reserves 26px of headroom for an 80px animation. That second cluster is exactly the "keep up the animations with the speed" and "visually satisfying at end game" risk you named — the code anticipated it for floats and stopped there.

One genuinely latent bug worth fixing before the sim is trusted further: `multiplier(st, 0)` returns the Hot-Hand-buffed value, the opposite of what `income()`'s own comment promises, and sim.js calls `E.income(st, taps, 0)` in ten places including the invariant-1 income-drop check.

Nothing in the spec marked in scope is missing. The value window, the near-circle tick modes, the 1→2 lens morph winding fix, the commit-on-release shop, the pending-before-SDK ad record and the v1 migration are all present and behave as written.

**Findings:**
- [major] **MAX ROLL readout excludes every multiplier, so it contradicts the floats it sits above** (index.html:665-706 (maxRoll), 2877 (setText #maxrollN), 2131 (float text)): `maxRoll(dice)` (index.html:665-706) is documented as "multipliers excluded" and takes only the dice array — no shards, no set bonus, no Hot Hand. But `roll()` pays `mul(v, m)` where `m = multiplier(st, nowMs)` (index.html:~905), and the per-die float prints `E.fmt(paid[i])` (index.html:2131). `G.maxRollTarget` is only ever fed from `E.maxRoll` (index.html:2208, 2256, 3203, 3227). After the first Recast `shardsFor` returns 12, so `globalMult` = 1 + 0.15*12 = 2.8 and every payout is at least 2.8x the number under the dice — before the +10%/+10% skin sets and the x3 Hot Hand, which together reach ~10x. The one permanent readout in the game is then simply false, and it is the readout the spec (sec 3.3) makes the progress meter. Suggested fix: Multiply the displayed target by `E.runMult(st)` (and by HOT_MULT while hot, matching the float tint), i.e. feed `E.maxRoll(st.dice) * E.multiplier(st, Date.now())` into `G.maxRollTarget`; or relabel the line so it is not read as "the biggest number you can see". Do the multiplication with ECON helpers, not in the renderer.
- [major] **Vibration, jackpot chime and jackpot sparkle fire once per roll at high roller levels** (index.html:2073-2100 (landResult), 2775-2803 (auto-roll tick)): `landResult` calls `buzz(18)` on every match (index.html:2092) and `G.sparkle = 0.9; Audio2.play('jack'); buzz([12,40,12,40,24])` on every jackpot landing (index.html:2099). Nothing throttles either. In the auto-roll block (index.html:2775-2803) `landResult(last)` runs on essentially every frame once `autoAcc` crosses 1 each frame — at roller L11, `autoRate = 2*1.35^11 ≈ 57.8` rolls/s, so ~58 landResults/s. With three dice carrying jackpots at n=20, P(any jackpot) = 1-(19/20)^3 ≈ 14%, i.e. ~8 jackpot landings/s: an 128 ms vibration pattern started 8 times a second is continuous buzzing, the three-oscillator jack chime overlaps itself permanently, and `G.sparkle` is re-armed faster than it decays (it decays at 1.0/s from 0.9) so the screen-space coin glint never turns off and becomes ambient noise. Doubles do the same with `buzz(18)` at up to ~10/s with two 6-side dice. The float path is throttled to 8/s (`G.floatBudget`, index.html:2139-2158); the haptic, audio and sparkle paths are not. Suggested fix: Put buzz(), Audio2.play('land'/'jack') and the sparkle re-arm behind the same budget that gates floats — e.g. only fire them when `G.floatBudget === 0` (the 8/s slot), and when merging, fire one jackpot cue for the whole merged batch rather than per landing.
- [major] **Auto-rolls resolved in the same frame are dropped from the float feed instead of merged** (index.html:2775-2803 (update auto-roll), 2139-2158 (payFeedback/flushMerged)): In `update()` the auto-roll tick does `for (i = 0; i < n; i++) last = E.roll(...)` and then only ever hands `last` to `landResult`/`G.pendingLand` (index.html:2782-2799). The other n-1 results are discarded before `payFeedback` ever sees them, so they are not added to `G.merge` (index.html:2139-2151) either. Spec sec 5 is explicit: "Floats throttled to 8/s; skipped payouts are merged and shown as one summed float." The merge only covers rolls that reached payFeedback, not rolls the frame loop threw away. `n = floor(autoAcc)` where `autoAcc += dt * autoRate`, so at roller L11 (57.8 rolls/s) n is 1 per frame at 60 fps but 2 at 30 fps and 6 after a 100 ms hiccup (dt is clamped to 0.1 in `frame`). On a phone rendering three 100-gons at 30 fps the coin counter climbs at twice the rate the floats account for, which is exactly the "show, don't tell" contract breaking under load. Suggested fix: Accumulate the skipped results as you roll them: keep a running `paid[]`/`types[]` sum over the n rolls in the tick and pass that summed pseudo-result to `payFeedback`, rather than passing only `last`.

- [major] **DOUBLES/TRIPLES burst fires on jackpot matches that multiply nothing** (C:\Users\Nate\Downloads\Dice Mobile Game\index.html:2230 (burst), :647 (comboFace), :604 and :617 (comboFor/matchedSet)): `comboFace()` (index.html:647-649) deliberately returns 0 on a jackpot face, so `roll()` at index.html:976-980 computes `v = values[d] + comboFace(die, i) * (w - 1)` — for a jackpot face the bonus term is 0 and the payout is unchanged.
